# Diwali Sales Analysis 🪔📊

A data analysis project focused on understanding customer purchasing behavior during the Diwali festival season using Python.  
The goal of this project is to extract meaningful business insights from sales data and identify high-value customer segments, top-performing product categories, and regional sales trends.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Diwali is one of the biggest shopping seasons in India. Businesses receive a huge number of orders across different product categories and customer groups.  
In this project, I performed **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on Diwali sales data to understand:

- Customer purchasing patterns
- Gender and age-wise spending trends
- State-wise sales performance
- Occupation-based purchasing behavior
- Best-selling product categories

The analysis was done using Python libraries such as **Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, and Seaborn**.

## 📊 Key Insights

### 👩 Women customers contributed more sales
Female customers made more purchases compared to male customers during the Diwali season.

### 🎯 Age group 26–35 spent the most
Customers aged between **26–35 years** were the most active buyers.

### 💍 Married women were major buyers
Married women showed higher purchasing behavior than unmarried customers.

### 🌍 Top performing states
The highest sales were recorded from:
- Uttar Pradesh
- Maharashtra
- Karnataka

### 💼 Top occupations by spending
Customers working in:
- IT Sector
- Healthcare
- Aviation

had the highest purchasing power.

### 🛍️ Most popular product categories
Top-selling categories included:
- Food
- Clothing & Apparel
- Electronics
